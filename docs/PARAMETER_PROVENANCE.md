# Parameter provenance

This document records where each numerical parameter of the simulation and of the onboard software comes from, how the value was obtained, and under which conditions it holds. Sections 1 to 8 follow the subsystems: vehicle and plant, navigation sensors, sonar and HoloOcean, separation and envelope thresholds, gate protocol (P2), formation (P3), the B2 acoustic channel, and the onboard acoustic envelope. Section 9 lists the known model idealisations, section 10 the changes since the audit reports, and section 11 (last) the open provenance issues.

Status: 2026-10-29, branch `b2-acoustic-aided`, working tree as read during the session (the latest code edit read is from 23:03), including edits that were not yet committed when the session started. **The values are those of the code, not those printed in the audit reports**; where they differ, the change is listed in section 10. Companion files: `config/parameter_sources.json` (machine-readable registry of the rows below), `references.bib` (all citation keys), `docs/SENSOR_DEGRADATION_REFERENCES.md` (literature behind the CLEAN, LOW and MEDIUM sensor profiles) and `docs/ACOUSTIC_CHANNEL_MODEL.md` (the B2 channel).

## 0. How to read this document

**Audit reports.** R1 (vehicle, navigation sensors, hydrodynamics), R2 (sonar model and HoloOcean sensor semantics) and R3 (variant B2 acoustic channel) were produced on 2026-10-29. The sources used as support were opened by the audits; numbers are copied as printed and located by table, section or page. References such as "R1 4.2" or "R3 P7" point to their sections. The reports are archived outside the repository (`HoloHybridAutomaton_archive/provenance_work/`). Sources that could not be opened are marked UNVERIFIED in the reports and are not used here as support.

**Evidence classes** (as in the reports): (a) manufacturer specification; (b) peer-reviewed field or tank measurement; (c) derivation, with the formula given; (d) calibration in our own simulator; (e) assumption without empirical support.

**Verdict classes.**

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SUPPORTED | a source of class (a) or (b) reports the value or range under conditions that map onto the model |
| PARTIALLY SUPPORTED | only part of the value is supported (an order of magnitude, a best case, the magnitudes but not the structure) |
| DERIVED (from X) | follows from a stated source or model by a formula, from a measurement in our simulator, or from a formal check |
| DESIGN CHOICE | chosen by the project without an external source; feasibility may be checked against sources |
| STRESS ASSUMPTION | deliberately harsh or arbitrary setting; the phenomenon may be documented qualitatively but no source gives the value |
| CONTRADICTED | a source or a computation contradicts the value or an assumption attached to it; the code action is stated in the row, and unresolved cases are listed in section 11 |

Mapping of report verdicts: R2 "MEASURED IN OUR SIMULATOR" is DERIVED (from simulator measurement); R2 "DESIGN ASSUMPTION" is DESIGN CHOICE; R1 "no support (simulator constants)" is DESIGN CHOICE (inherited default); R1 "omission" is DESIGN CHOICE with an open issue. Where the verdict of the reports differs by level (for example LOW and MEDIUM), the levels have separate rows.

**Columns.** *Value*: the current code value with its location (file and qualified name); level-dependent values are given as CLEAN / LOW / MEDIUM. *Source*: the verdict class followed by the evidence. *DOI or URL*: identifiers of the entries in the *Citation key* column (DOI, arXiv number or URL) and, for simulator measurements, the repository file. *Derivation*: the mapping from source to value. *Validity*: conditions and limits. The registry keeps the same rows in the same order. "DI-n" refers to the numbered entries of `DESIGN_ITERATIONS.md` at the repository root.

## 1. Vehicle and plant

The simulated plant is HoloOcean 2.3.0 default dynamics for the BlueROV2, calibrated in closed loop (class d). It should not be called a BlueROV2 hydrodynamic model: the two experimental drag sources differ by a factor of about 3.6, and the isotropic engine drag cannot represent the measured anisotropy. The simulated horizontal thrust is about 0.15 (nominal authority) to 0.4 (full command) of a real Heavy.

## 2. Navigation sensors

CLEAN is the deployed suite; LOW and MEDIUM are the realistic operating domain of the validation campaign (`sim/sensor_noise.py`). Heading is `atan2(m_y, m_x)` of the raw magnetometer sample, the DVL body velocity is rotated by it and integrated, and depth comes from the pressure sensor; gyro and accelerometer do not enter the horizontal position estimate (`perception/nav.py`). The literature behind these values is summarised in `docs/SENSOR_DEGRADATION_REFERENCES.md`.

## 9. Known model idealisations

These are documented limits of the simulator or of the model structure, not conflicts between a value and a source.

- **Sonar ranges are geometric.** HoloOcean computes the Euclidean distance to the octree leaf; there is no sound-speed range error, no transmission loss, no beam pattern and no refraction. The bin value is the mean of R^2 cos over the leaves, so detection is range-independent up to `RangeMax` and uniform up to the cone edge; the water surface is not in the octree and is invisible (`docs/v2/SONAR_PROBE.md`). A sound-speed mismatch of 20 to 50 m/s would add a scale error of 1.3 to 3.4 % (+0.03 to +0.08 m at 2.4 m, +0.16 to +0.40 m at 12 m, DOSITS sensitivities); a hardware deployment needs c to about +-0.5 %.
- **Rayleigh intensity noise** is optimistic against shallow-water field data (heavier tails). No acoustic interference between the 36 sonars of a six-drone fleet is modelled.
- **Plant:** no added mass, no thruster deadzone or lag; isotropic drag.
- **Compass:** the simulated magnetic vector is horizontal (no tilt coupling); the bias is constant per run, without heading dependence or time variation.
- **Acoustic channel:** losses are independent per reception with a linear range ramp, while measured loss is position-dependent and correlated; the clock offset is constant per run (a TCXO-class clock or a mission beyond 1 h needs offset plus drift).
- **B2 hardware** is research grade (section 7 and `docs/ACOUSTIC_CHANNEL_MODEL.md`), not a COTS feature.

## 11. Open provenance issues

Each item was checked against the working tree on 2026-10-29: the parameter named is unchanged unless stated. Items O-1 to O-7 and O-11 come from the audit reports; O-8 and O-12 were found while compiling this document; O-9 and O-10 are documentation drift.

**O-1. MEDIUM sonar range jitter against the declared `eps_range`.** `sensor_noise.MEDIUM.sonar_range_jitter_m` = 0.03 m shifts the whole profile by k = round(N(0, 0.03) / 0.05 m) bins (`holo_env._degrade`, no truncation). P(abs(k) >= 2) = 6.2e-3 per side. On the measured hull baselines (+0.044 to +0.054 m far side, -0.006 m near side) a shift of two bins gives +0.144 to +0.154 m and -0.106 m, while `Envelope.eps_range_far` = 0.12 m and `eps_range_near` = 0.08 m; the envelope would need about 0.154 m and 0.106 m. About 1.2 % of MEDIUM captures violate it for hull echoes (0.62 % per side); LOW is consistent (3e-7 per side). The anchor behind the jitter (Ping resolution 0.5 % of range) is a misread and does not support the value. *Code state*: values unchanged; the exceedance is now declared in the `sensor_noise.py` docstring and in the comment of `Envelope.eps_range_far` (MEDIUM is a stress level outside the proved P1 envelope). *Open*: nobody has yet counted how often a guard used such a capture. *Action* (R2 3b): report that count, or truncate the jitter at one bin for the validation levels.

**O-2. `structure_tol_far_m` against the MEDIUM `range_sigma`.** `PerceptionConfig.structure_tol_far_m` = 0.70 m was derived for range_sigma 0.05 m (0.05 ln N, about 0.4 m for N = 10^3 leaves, DI-14). MEDIUM uses 0.10 m: the expected maximum offset over 100 / 300 / 1000 leaves is 0.52 / 0.63 / 0.75 m, and the probability that at least one leaf is pushed beyond 0.70 m is 0.09 / 0.24 / 0.60 per capture (8e-5 to 8e-4 at 0.05 m). A structure segment that passes `sw[1] + 0.70` fails `in_struct` (`sonar_processing.py:185`) and becomes UNKNOWN or DYNAMIC at the structure's own range. Not verified on logs. *Action* (R2 2d): replay logged MEDIUM profiles near gates with the tolerance scaled to range_sigma before attributing unexplained bar echoes to navigation drift.

**O-3. DVL measurement age.** One `latency_s` (0.05 s at MEDIUM) applies to every onboard stream (`holo_env._tick`). The sources give 0.1 s (von Benzon Table 1) and 0.189 s (one example in the Water Linked documentation) between time of validity and delivery. Effect: a dead-reckoning offset v tau of 0.03 to 0.057 m at 0.30 m/s and additional phase lag in the DVL velocity loop. *Action* (R1 9.1): use 0.1 to 0.2 s for the DVL stream at MEDIUM, stamped with the time of validity.

**O-4. No DVL scale factor.** `NoiseProfile` and `holo_env._degrade` contain white noise, dropouts and outages for the DVL but no long-term scale error. The A50 Standard is specified at +-1.01 %, the license-free Nortek at above 1 %, and the Wayfinder measured within +-1.15 %: up to 1 % of the distance travelled along track (0.3 m over a 30 m approach). *Action* (R1 4.3): a per-run, per-drone scale factor uniform in +-1 % for LOW and MEDIUM, or an explicit statement of the omission.

**O-5. LOW heading bias 0.5 deg is a best case.** `NoiseProfile.heading_bias_deg` = 0.5 deg (LOW). Calibrated compasses reach 1 to 3 deg (Kinsey); MMC5983MA 1.0 deg typical; MAVBE about 1 deg; 0.55 deg after MAGYC in one experiment. *Action* (R1 6.1): raise LOW to 1.0 deg or label it "best-case in-situ calibration".

**O-6. MEDIUM navigation error against `nav_err_gate_m` = 1.0 m.** `AcousticConfig.nav_err_gate_m` is the own position error assumed in the gate frame. A 2 deg heading bias reaches 1.0 m after a 28.6 m approach (27.5 m with a 1 % scale factor); R1 reports MEDIUM runs at 0.7 to 1.1 m, so the top end exceeds the assumption. The map aiding bounds the drift only inside the acquisition region (4.24 m) and only when a fit is accepted. *Action* (R1 8): cap the approach distance used in the gate argument, or raise the value to about 1.2 m if the DVL scale factor is added.

**O-7. `tau_max` has no margin at MEDIUM.** `Envelope.tau_max` = 0.25 s; MEDIUM latency 0.05 s, capture period 0.1 s, dropout 1 %. R1: the newest sonar data are up to 0.15 s old just before the next capture and exactly 0.25 s after one dropped capture; R2 reaches the same boundary after two consecutive drops (1e-4 at 1 %) with a different age convention. The test is `age <= tau_max` (`sonar_processing.py:169`), a floating-point boundary. A MEDIUM blackout of 0.15 s exceeds `tau_max` by design (the sector becomes unhealthy, FAILSAFE is exercised); the `sensor_noise.py` docstring now states that MEDIUM exceeds `tau_max` with its outages. *Action* (R1 9.2): state that MEDIUM has no margin, or raise `tau_max` together with the staleness argument. EchoHold (commit 31efb0d) holds the nearest echo of every healthy sector for `tau_max`.

**O-8. `heading_err_deg` = 3.0 deg covers the MEDIUM compass bias but less than one standard deviation of its noise.** `AcousticConfig.heading_err_deg` is "compass bias plus noise" for a MEDIUM bias of 2.0 deg, leaving 1.0 deg for noise. The heading used for a fix is the raw per-sample compass value (`nav.py`), whose MEDIUM noise is 57.3 x 0.02 = 1.15 deg (1 sigma, R1 6.2). With the envelope's own convention (k_sigma = 3) the bound would be 2.0 + 3 x 1.15 = 5.4 deg. Derived here from R1 and the code; not checked on logs. The envelope sums worst cases of independent terms, so the combined exceedance may still be small.

**O-11. MEDIUM channel against the one-way field figures.** R3 P1 and P4: the MEDIUM white errors (range 0.15 m, direction 2.5 deg) lie at the datasheet end. The array bias and the clear-path episodes added since R3 narrow the gap in azimuth (the 68 % quantile is at most 4.2 deg at MEDIUM, computed here), not in range. *Action* (R3 P1, P4): a sensitivity level with range sigma of 0.5 to 0.7 m and direction sigma of 3 to 7 deg, and the requirement that the sender's trigger jitter is much smaller than 30 us.
