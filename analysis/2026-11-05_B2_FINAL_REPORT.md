# B2 consolidation of HoloHybridAutomaton: final report

Branch `b2-consolidation`; frozen implementation and criteria: tag `b2-freeze-v10` (commit `9866242`).
Campaign results: `results/b2/final_v10/SUMMARY.md` (generated by `scripts/analyze_b2final.py`) and
`results/b2/final_v10/MECHANISMS.md` (generated by `scripts/failure_mechanisms.py`).

## 1. Scope

A leaderless fleet of BlueROV2 vehicles in HoloOcean 2.3.0 (patched sonar octree) runs one local hybrid automaton
per drone (10 modes, 130 edges, `holo_fleet/ha/spec.py`), with six single-beam sonars, DVL, IMU, compass and depth.
There is no coordinator.  Variant B2, the only operational architecture, adds a one-way acoustic beacon.  The
report covers the consolidation from the B2-only review (`analysis/2026-11-03_B2_REVIEW_AND_PLAN.md`) to the frozen
validation campaign.

Properties (definitions fixed before any result and never changed):

* **P1** the true distance between any two drones stays at or above d_safe = 1.0 m, with no physical contact;
* **P2** at most one drone centre is inside the gate critical region (CR, |s| <= 0.8 m, |l| <= 1.0 m,
  |dz| <= 1.0 m) at any time, and the first drone of the queue order leads;
* **P3** the formation error is at most 1.0 m (inclusive) for 8.9 s and every loss episode recovers; **P3-deg**:
  after a permanent fault the healthy drones keep their original slots, every drone that expects the lost one
  declares its slot vacant, and no slot is falsely vacant.

## 2. What changed, and why

### 2.1 Evaluation (criteria v4, `holo_fleet/referee/`)

* **Pre-registration.** Requirements and deadlines are derived from the mission plan, the protocol bounds and the
  sensor-profile definitions (`criteria.py`), never from runs of the system under test.  Each constant carries a
  provenance label [REQ], [PROT], [LIT], [PROF] or [CAL].
* **Gate events.** E1-E8 follow the STPA notion of an unsafe control action (Leveson 2012,
  doi:10.7551/mitpress/8179.001.0001).  Gate liveness is a bounded-response property (Dwyer et al. 1999,
  doi:10.1145/302405.302672), with the crossing obligation counted from the first commit.
* **Classes.** UNSAFE_ACTION covers E2.to_free, E3 and E4.  SAFE_DEGRADED is causal and requires the hold.  The
  offline replay receives the logged contacts and the resolved fault onsets, and the ground-truth safety items are
  judged on every complete run whatever its class.
* **Completion times.** Kaplan-Meier curves censored at the deadline (doi:10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452).

### 2.2 The observability limit that motivated H6

An executed counterexample from the Codex phase (`results/dev/issue3/indistinguishable_occupancy.json`) showed a
basic limit of range-bearing evidence.  A drone at s = 0.8 m with a +3 m multipath excess, which lies inside the
declared envelope, produces the same accepted fixes indefinitely as a drone at s = 3.8 m without excess.  A veto
that is complete over the envelope must therefore veto both.

The full-region veto was safe but stalled the CLEAN gate scenario: the queued drones behind the first one looked
possibly inside.  The earlier clear-path veto, written in the previous session, was unsound
(`results/dev/issue3/multipath_counterexample.json`).

### 2.3 H6: one-way position payload (`perception/payload.py`, `perception/nav.py`)

* **Packet.** Every ping carries 56 bits: a 2-bit TDMA frame, the sender's onboard mission-frame position (12 + 12
  bits at 5 cm, depth 8 bits), an error-ball bound (6 bits at 0.1 m, 63 = invalid) and CRC-16/CCITT.
* **Strictly one-way.** No request, reply, acknowledgement, mode or control decision is sent.  The data come from
  the sender's own estimate (`Perception.payload`), never from the simulator.
* **Bound (NavBound).** The sum of worst-case terms of the declared MEDIUM navigation envelope:
  - launch survey, 0.30 m;
  - compass bias and DVL scale error proportional to the distance travelled;
  - integrated white DVL noise at the 99.73 % level of a 2-D normal;
  - a held-velocity term during DVL outages, with a_max = 1.5 m/s^2 from the calibrated reversal;
  - every applied map correction;
  - the DVL lever arm, sample hold and latency.

  It never decreases.  A correction larger than the bound marks it invalid.  The ball radius is
  hypot(horizontal, vertical).
* **Air time (stated assumption H6, unverified on hardware).** At 1 kbit/s net the packet takes 56 ms.  With the
  identity chirp as preamble and the 33 ms propagation spread at 50 m, a slot needs 129 ms of the 167 ms
  available to n = 6 drones at a 1 s period.  Modems with short-range rates above 1 kbit/s exist (EvoLogics
  S2CM-HS, Subnero M25M, Teledyne Benthos DAT), but none documents this frame time on a synchronised TDMA
  schedule.  A 64 bit/s BlueROV2-class modem cannot carry it at this period.
* **Fusion.** A payload is used only if its frame matches the schedule and its ball is consistent with the
  range-bearing region grown by both bounds.  The gate rules use the intersection of the two regions: a drone is
  clear if either region is clear of the CR, and a possible occupant only if both allow it.  A randomized check
  B2-P confirms that a sender truly in the CR, with its error inside the sent bound, is never cleared.  Its
  mutation B2-Pm shows that the radius max(h, z) would clear one.
* **Unknown occupant.** A drone whose latest accepted fixes placed it possibly in the CR keeps the latch BUSY until
  exit_confirm cleared fixes arrive.  It also holds the stage-2 entry; the t_occ_max fallback never releases it.
* **Relocalization beside the gate opening.** A fit there is applied only when the payload balls of every other
  drone exclude every echo, evaluated at the uncorrected pose with the own attitude error.  Such a fit never
  validates the localization.
* **Formation clocks.** The neighbours' reported positions, compared with where the own formation time places
  their slots, drive the lag, the "ahead" veto and the displacement rule.  This is a first-order progress
  consensus (Ghabcheloo et al. 2009, doi:10.1137/060678993).

### 2.4 Explicit give-up of a gate entry (Issue 1)

* **Edge.** `pass_abort` (MUTEX_PASS to MUTEX_APPROACH, committed := false) is guarded by a new observation,
  `entry_abort`, with invariant I5.  The flow requests it only after the bounded stage-2 hold (t_occ_max + t_clear),
  only for a drone never admitted, and only behind the plane s = -(cr_half_len + R_OUT + 1.0 m).
* **Proofs.** The formal properties were restated as G2 and G2c.  Mutation MA reintroduces the withdrawn flow-only
  retreat, which kept the commitment, and yields a counterexample.
* **Livelock.** A persistent stage-2 cause still repeats commit, hold and give-up.  The referee classifies such a
  run as STALLED.

### 2.5 New scenario

`gate_stalled_occupant` is gate_single without current.  The first drone loses its thrusters once its centre is in
the second half of the CR; the trigger reads ground truth on the simulator side only.

The v10 campaign has three cells (protocol / sensor profile), fixed by the owner after v6 (criteria v7):

| cell | protocol / sensor profile | role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | B2_CLEAN / CLEAN | reference |
| 2 | B2_LOW / LOW-ALL | nominal conditions; must pass |

* **Runs.** 12 non-gate scenarios x seeds 800-802 and 6 gate scenarios x seeds 800-805, i.e. 72 runs per cell and

* **Execution.** Low priority, from worktrees of the frozen commit (never from the development checkout).  From

  Processes outside the campaign terminated our orchestrators four times and our workers repeatedly (exit code 1,

  runs repeated; none remains INVALID.

### 4.1 Results (`results/b2/final_v10/SUMMARY.md`)

* **Safety (item 1).** In the 216 complete runs (108 at a gate) there is no P1 or P2 violation, no contact and no
  unsafe action (E2.to_free, E3, E4).  The 95 % upper bound of the per-run rate is 0.0139 (rule of three).  The
  smallest true separation is 2.491 m in CLEAN, 2.478 m in LOW-ALL and 2.533 m in MEDIUM-ALL (d_safe = 1.0 m).  The
  offline replay matches every run (REPLAY_MISMATCH 0).
* **H6 premise (item 2).** The sent navigation bound contained the true error in all 216 runs; the smallest margin
  is 0.956 m.
* **Liveness by cell (item 4; gate_stalled_occupant excluded, 66 runs per cell).**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  (3 x the deadline).  "PASS late at the gate" counts the PASS runs whose gate crossing missed its bounded-response
  time after the first commit.  No run was SAFE_DEGRADED.  The aggregator's heading says "16 scenarios"; the cells hold 17

* **gate_stalled_occupant (item 5).** 18 runs, all accepted on safety (P1, P2, no contact, E2.to_free = E3 = E4 =

  McNemar comparison has no pairs.

The closure condition is met: CLEAN and nominal LOW-ALL are PASS in every run, and safety holds in every run of
every cell.

### 4.2 The ten MEDIUM-ALL INCOMPLETE runs (`results/b2/final_v10/MECHANISMS.md`)

All ten are liveness failures at the end of the horizon, with P1 and P2 intact.  At the end of each, every drone's
navigation bound lies between 6 and 20 m and no drone has a valid localization (`loc_ok` false).

* **lost_drone_mutex, 4 of 6 seeds (801-804).** Drone 0 fails as scripted and retreats.  One survivor declares its
  slot vacant; the other does not.  That survivor ends in FORMATION_RECOVERY (s802-s804) or MUTEX_YIELD (s801), and
  by design the vacancy timer runs only while a drone holds its own slot in a formation mode
  (`perception/perception.py`: a drone that is itself lost or avoiding cannot judge the others).  The slot is
  therefore never declared vacant ("P3-deg vacancy" missing).  In s801 the gate is not passed either, and one survivor
  holds the other survivor's slot vacant (a false vacancy).
* **integrated_short, 3 of 6 seeds.** In s800 and s803 the queued drones stay in MUTEX_YIELD: the acoustic fixes
  cannot confirm that a possible occupant is outside, so the latch stays BUSY, and the drones give up an entry once
  or twice.  In s800 a drone also holds both other slots vacant.  This is the conservative direction of the unknown-occupant rule (Section 2.3).  In s804 the committed
  drone is held in SEPARATION_WARNING 3.55 m before the gate with a 19.7 m bound, and the other two hold its slot
  vacant (a false vacancy).
* **formation_gust s800, formation_recovery_head_current s800, lost_drone_rejoin s800.** The formation does not
  re-form.  With bounds of 6.6-9.7 m the drones mark present neighbours missing, and some hold a present neighbour's
  slot vacant.  In lost_drone_rejoin s800 one drone is still waiting for its neighbour at the end.

Nothing was changed after the freeze.  Letting a drone outside the formation modes time a neighbour's absence would be a
behaviour change and would need a new freeze; it is recorded as a limit (Section 5).

## 5. Limits stated, not hidden

* **Campaign scope.** Three cells: CLEAN, LOW-ALL and MEDIUM-ALL.  There is no total acoustic blackout and no HIGH
  profile, so neither the acoustic benefit (item 6) nor the behaviour beyond MEDIUM is measured.  With no safety failure,
  the 95 % upper bound of the per-run rate is 0.0139 over the 216 runs and 0.0417 within one 72-run cell.
* **P1.** Proved for both drones of a pair braking.  For one-sided braking it rests on the simulator calibration
  (0.743 m/s^2).  Under LOW and MEDIUM it is tested, not proved, because the echo envelope is exceeded.
  Assumption (D), a hard-edged sonar cone, is a simulator idealisation.
* **P2.** Rests on two assumptions:
  - the sonar-latch premises (M3), with the exceptions measured in the earlier campaigns;
  - H6: every sender's navigation bound holds.  It is logged against the truth in every run (smallest margin
    0.956 m in v10); the HIGH profile lies outside it.

  A silent stalled occupant in the masked far half of the CR under an acoustic BLACKOUT is outside the guarantee
  (M3f, M4h).  Only about 16 % of the CR is observable from the hold point.
* **Deployment assumption (v8).** A completely failed drone is taken to be net positively buoyant: a silent drone
  last known in the CR is considered gone after max(t_occ_max + t_clear, 50 s).  A drone that fails without rising
  (entangled, negatively ballasted) would block the gate permanently, the safe direction.
* **Vertical pass (v9).** A drone that rejoins a slot with another slot behind it on its lane passes 3.5 m below
  (above without depth room).  It relies on the modelled up and down obstacle sonars, which real BlueROV2s rarely
  carry, and on depth room: the pass level of -8.5 m is the band edge z_min + 0.5 m, and at a shallow site the
  seabed would limit it.
* **Liveness.**
  - Under MEDIUM-ALL, 56 of 66 runs pass (Wilson 95 % interval 0.74-0.92).  In the ten INCOMPLETE runs the
    navigation bounds reach 6-20 m (Section 4.2).
  - A drone outside the formation modes (FORMATION_RECOVERY, MUTEX_YIELD) does not time a neighbour's absence, so
    after a permanent loss a survivor that cannot hold its own slot never declares the lost drone's slot vacant
    (4 of the 10 runs).
  - Under the unknown-occupant rule an unconfirmed exit keeps the gate latch BUSY, the safe direction, and the
    give-up edge does not exclude a livelock.
  - The navigation bound grows with distance and DVL outages, so the payload clears less often on long or noisy
    missions.
* **P3.** The ranking functions F1-F3 concern each drone's estimated slot; true-formation recovery is empirical.
  Transient false vacancies occur in PASS runs of every cell (1, 4 and 2 runs).
* **Reproducibility.** HoloOcean is not bit-deterministic: its own sensor noise is unseeded, and the same seed has
  given PASS and then INCOMPLETE in development.  Before/after pairings are therefore nominal.  The 84 attempts
  interrupted by external processes in v10 had no verdict and were repeated; no run with a verdict was repeated.
* **Hardware.** H1-H6 are research-grade assumptions.  The payload loss is a stress parameter, not a measured
  rate.
