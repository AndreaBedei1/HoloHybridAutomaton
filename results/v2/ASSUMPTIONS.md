A1 counts every control step (after the first two captures) at which another hull, drone or scripted vehicle, is within 5 m of a drone: the guards can be affected only below d_warning_exit plus the largest looseness of the bound (2.7 + 1.2 m).  Between 5 and 8 m a hull may be missed for a capture (weak returns) or masked by the gate bars; those cases are counted separately and do not concern P1. Masked by structure: a hull within 5 m whose echo fell inside the window of a mapped structure (the gate frame), so it was classified STRUCTURE: in mutex_deadlock_resolution a passing drone near the gate frame does not see the queued drone below it or on its right for about 1.2 s in total (12 control steps). That is the known limit 'mapped structure masks drones' (README section 9); the separation in that interval rests on the pass-path geometry (formal M4s: clearance >= 3.5 m from the queue points still occupied), true minimum 3.44 m.

A2: the age of the sonar data used by the guards (0 when every capture arrives; tau_max covers one dropped capture). A4: the closest pair of drones that are both holding their queue points. A5: the along-axis speed of the drone inside the critical region (formal M3 needs >= 0.12 m/s).

E1 judges the true current (logged field at the true positions) against what each drone was asked to deliver: on its body axes, the steady command g(r_surge), g(r_sway) needed for the through-water velocity r = v_requested - w must stay within the authority of its mode (0.40 nominal), with the plant curve g(r) = |r|/2.4 + 0.203 r^2 calibrated in results/calibration/head_current_authority.json (tolerance 6 %), persistently (the onboard 4 s rule); and the current must stay within the exercised range. Head (positive: against the motion) and lateral are the components of the current relative to the requested motion at the binding step, the step where the required command peaks. E2 is the drone's own view: ENVELOPE_VIOLATION when it cannot deliver the requested velocity for 4 s (persistent saturation, or its own steady command beyond the authority). integrated_short: until v2.1 its 0.35 m/s cross-current gust put drone 0's diagonal gate passage at 0.5 m/s at up to 1.12 times the nominal authority for about 5 s (outside, not declared onboard: a lag, not a persistent saturation, DI-27); since DI-31 the gust is 0.27 m/s (only the scenario changed) and the passage stays within the authority. FAILSAFE steps include the ones caused by a declaration. In the lost-drone scenarios the drone whose thrusters fail (simulator fault) declares ENVELOPE_VIOLATION itself: that declaration is not about the current and is not counted against E1; its FAILSAFE lasts until it moves again as asked (DI-29).
