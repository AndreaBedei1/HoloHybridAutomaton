E1 judges the true current (logged field at the true positions) against what each drone was asked to deliver: on its body axes, the steady command g(r_surge), g(r_sway) needed for the through-water velocity r = v_requested - w must stay within the authority of its mode (0.40 nominal), with the plant curve g(r) = |r|/2.4 + 0.203 r^2 calibrated in results/calibration/head_current_authority.json (tolerance 6 %), persistently (the onboard 4 s rule); and the current must stay within the exercised range. Head (positive: against the motion) and lateral are the components of the current relative to the requested motion at the binding step, the step where the required command peaks. E2 is the drone's own view: ENVELOPE_VIOLATION when it cannot deliver the requested velocity for 4 s (persistent saturation, or its own steady command beyond the authority). integrated_short: drone 0's diagonal gate passage at 0.5 m/s against the 0.30 m/s cross-current needs up to 1.12 times the nominal authority for about 5 s; the drone delivers its maximum (ratio 1.00 on the achieved velocity) with a lag of up to 0.07 m/s and its command stays at the edge without persistent saturation, so its own monitor does not declare it (DESIGN_ITERATIONS.md, DI-27). FAILSAFE steps include the ones caused by a declaration.
