Held-out validation of the controller with the gate guard (branch review-p2-closure), written 2026-11-17 before any
held-out run and before reading the adversarial results.

Change under test: the gate guard of the avoidance layer (holo_fleet/control/flows.gate_guard, control/escape.py).  It acts
only on a drone at a gate, inside the keep-out box, not admitted; outside gate passages the controller is unchanged, so the
affected properties are P1 and P2 at the gate and the gate liveness (P3 at the gate re-formation).

Scenarios (every gate scenario of the final campaign that exercises the queue, the hold and the avoidance at the gate):
  gate_single, mutex_deadlock_resolution, line_parallel_mutex, lost_drone_mutex, st_lost_middle, integrated_short.
Protocols and profiles: B2_MEDIUM with MEDIUM-ALL (the deployed configuration), B0 with LOW-ALL (communication-free).
Seeds: 7300, 7301, 7302, 7303, 7304 (never used before; consecutive, fixed now).  6 x 2 x 5 = 60 runs.
Targeted complement (the conditions of s5201): st_lost_middle, B2_MEDIUM, MEDIUM-ALL, seeds 7310-7319 (10 runs).

Criteria, fixed now:
  * P1 and P2 hold in every run (referee, ground truth); any violation is reported, never excluded;
  * no CR entry with another drone inside; every CR entry not admitted (PUSHED, UNCOMMITTED) is reported with its guard
    events, and every GATE_GUARD_OVERRIDE is reported;
  * premise C classes (event audit) reported per protocol; VIOLATED pairs are reported with their run;
  * liveness: the class of every run (PASS, P3_LATE, INCOMPLETE, ...) compared with the final campaign of b2-freeze-v11 for
    the same scenarios and protocols (rates, not paired seeds); a drop is reported as a cost of the guard.
Runs are executed once; an INVALID run (infrastructure) is re-run with the same seed and reported.
